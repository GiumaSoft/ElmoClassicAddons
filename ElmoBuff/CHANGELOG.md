# 1.0.2

Added shared handling of mutually exclusive buff families. All learned, enabled
alternatives appear before a buff is confirmed; an active conflicting buff hides
the alternatives only for that member. Removed the one-blessing selector in
favor of individual buff checkboxes. Paladins can choose Might, Wisdom or Kings
directly beside each member. Normal/Greater blessings, caster ownership,
expiration, early refresh and unreadable aura data are respected. Compatible
Priest, Mage and Druid buff families keep their existing independent behavior.

# 1.0.1

Initial public release for Classic Era and WoW Forever. Click missing-buff icons beside native party/player frames to buff the corresponding member without changing target. Supports Priest, Mage, Druid and Paladin buffs. Existing group versions, different ranks and foreign buffs suppress unnecessary replacements; early refresh applies only to your own exact cast rank. Includes Options > AddOns settings for buffs, icon position, size and refresh threshold. Rebuff suggestions and clicks are available outside combat; raids and pets are excluded.
