# 1.0.0

Initial release for Classic Era and WoW Forever. Detects living rare and rare-elite NPCs exposed through nameplates, target or mouseover, with sound and a hover tooltip. Includes Options > AddOns settings and an optional key to place a skull on the current target through Blizzard's secure raidtarget action. Detection is limited to units exposed by the client; it is not a map-wide or dungeon-wide spawn scan.
